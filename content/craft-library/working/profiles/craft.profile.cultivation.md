## What it is

Cultivation fiction structures power growth as internal refinement toward
transcendence, through named realms, dramatized breakthroughs, sect
politics, and alchemy/refinement practices. The path to power is
conventionally also a path of self-understanding, distinguishing this
subgenre from purely mechanical progression systems.

## Why readers may care

The promise is that breakthroughs are events with meaning, not just
threshold crossings — readers of this subgenre are reported to care more
about *why* a character advances than about the raw numbers involved, the
inverse of classic LitRPG's number-forward appeal. That inversion is the
single most important thing this profile changes about how other library
patterns should be read.

## Common forms and variants

Cultivation ranges from close adaptations of Chinese xianxia/xuanhuan
conventions to Western cultivation fiction, which conventionally compresses
pacing relative to the source tradition and establishes stakes within the
first few pages — a genre convention associated with Will Wight's influence
on the Western tradition specifically, per genre research, not a claim
about the wider tradition's own pacing norms.

## What this profile modifies in other records

- **Cultivation realms and breakthroughs** is this profile's companion
  system-mechanic record and should be read alongside it directly.
- **Qi versus mana** applies with qi (in its modern genre-convention sense,
  distinguished from historical Daoist usage) as the default resource
  framing.
- **Meaningless numbers** inverts in framing: the failure here is
  unexplained advancement (a breakthrough with no stated reason) rather than
  weightless numbers specifically, since numbers are frequently secondary or
  absent.
- **Advancement rate**'s widening-interval shape is the explicit convention:
  later realms take longer, and pacing should slow deliberately rather than
  reading as stalled.

## What it can look like on the page

- A breakthrough scene dramatized with genuine risk, insight, or moral
  weight, not just a threshold crossing.
- Sect politics, alchemy, or philosophical questions given narrative space
  proportional to their conventional centrality in the subgenre.
- The inverse, as a warning sign: realm changes announced with mechanical
  flatness, with no accompanying qualitative or philosophical development.

## Characteristic failure mode

Breakthroughs become mechanical and the philosophical half goes vestigial —
realms function as levels with better names, losing the reason-over-numbers
appeal this subgenre's readers are reported to want.

## Revision or design experiments

1. For each realm change, write one sentence describing what qualitatively
   or philosophically changed, not just what number or rank changed.
2. Check whether sect politics or a mentor-student relationship carries
   real narrative weight, or functions only as background texture.
3. Compare the pacing of a late-book realm to an early one; if the later one
   moves at the same pace, consider whether slowing it (per genre
   convention) would strengthen the sense of escalating difficulty.

## Questions for the author

- What does a breakthrough mean for this character beyond a numeric or
  ranked increase?
- Is the philosophical or spiritual dimension of cultivation given narrative
  space, or has it become vestigial?
- Which transliteration and tradition is "qi" (or its equivalent) drawing
  from in this manuscript, and is that legible to the reader?

## When this profile does not apply

A numeric, mechanically-focused power system with no qualitative or
philosophical dimension to its advancement is better served by the classic-
LitRPG or progression-fantasy profile; applying cultivation's
reasons-over-numbers framing to such a system would ask it to deliver a
promise it isn't making.

## Evidence and detection limits

Whether a manuscript uses named realms and breakthrough events is directly
observable from its structure. Whether breakthroughs carry genuine
qualitative or philosophical weight, rather than functioning as reskinned
level-ups, requires interpreting the prose and remains model-assisted.

## Original micro-examples

*Vestigial philosophy:* A character reaches Core Formation described purely
in terms of increased power output, with no accompanying change in outlook,
relationships, or self-understanding.

*Present philosophy:* The same breakthrough instead forces the character to
confront a compromise they made to survive an earlier ordeal, and the realm
change is explicitly tied to finally being honest with themselves about it.
