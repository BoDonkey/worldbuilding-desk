# Competitor Market Research Work Slices

## Executive summary

The five work slices below preserve the final five actions from the existing competitor-market research plan and translate them into implementation-ready units for another LLM. They move from direct competitor auditing, through broader market and growth validation, into differentiation analysis and final strategic synthesis. Priority reflects suggested execution order, with **1 as highest/earliest**; timelines and team size are assumed to be unspecified.

## Action summary

| Action | Priority |
|---|---:|
| Audit World Anvil's product, pricing, and community features from official sources. | 1 |
| Survey NovelCrafter and other writing tools for lore-building capabilities. | 2 |
| Analyze growth signals and community activity across social platforms. | 3 |
| Compare unique moats like temporal canon graphs and feature differentiation. | 4 |
| Summarize findings into actionable insights and recommended next steps. | 5 |

## Action details

**Audit World Anvil's product, pricing, and community features from official sources.**  
**Deliverables:** Current World Anvil feature inventory; pricing/tier comparison; breakdown of lore-building, campaign/gaming, publishing, collaboration, and community capabilities; concise assessment of its strongest competitive advantages.  
**Estimated effort:** Medium  
**Priority:** 1  
**Assumptions/dependencies:** Requires access to current World Anvil product pages, documentation, pricing, and official community materials. Establishes a baseline for later feature and moat comparisons.

**Survey NovelCrafter and other writing tools for lore-building capabilities.**  
**Deliverables:** Updated NovelCrafter assessment; shortlist of additional relevant competitors; comparison of lore databases, codex/worldbuilding systems, relationship management, chronology/timeline functionality, AI integration, and writing-workflow integration.  
**Estimated effort:** High  
**Priority:** 2  
**Assumptions/dependencies:** Competitor scope should emphasize products materially relevant to lore/worldbuilding rather than generic writing software. Builds on the World Anvil baseline and prior NovelCrafter research where available.

**Analyze growth signals and community activity across social platforms.**  
**Deliverables:** Evidence-based growth-signal comparison covering community size/activity, social engagement, creator visibility, release momentum, gaming/writing-community traction, and other observable indicators; identification of competitors that appear to be gaining momentum.  
**Estimated effort:** High  
**Priority:** 3  
**Assumptions/dependencies:** Public signals are proxies rather than verified company growth metrics unless revenue, subscriber, or usage figures are disclosed. Depends on the competitor shortlist produced by the preceding survey.

**Compare unique moats like temporal canon graphs and feature differentiation.**  
**Deliverables:** Differentiation matrix; assessment of which competitor capabilities are commodity versus defensible; gap analysis around temporal canon, entity relationships, chronology, continuity, and structured lore; prioritized moat opportunities.  
**Estimated effort:** Medium  
**Priority:** 4  
**Assumptions/dependencies:** Depends on the feature audits and competitor survey being sufficiently complete to distinguish genuine whitespace from features already available elsewhere.

**Summarize findings into actionable insights and recommended next steps.**  
**Deliverables:** Concise competitive-market synthesis; ranked threats and emerging competitors; product implications; recommended areas to defend, match, ignore, or differentiate; concrete next research or product actions.  
**Estimated effort:** Medium  
**Priority:** 5  
**Assumptions/dependencies:** Depends on completion of the preceding competitor, growth, and differentiation work; recommendations should clearly distinguish observed evidence from inference.
