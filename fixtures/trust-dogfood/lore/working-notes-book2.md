Loose planning notes. Nothing in here is decided. Half of it contradicts

## Backstory ideas I'm circling

- Earlier draft idea (probably abandoned): Sera was a smuggler before the
  Ledger-marks — ran chart-fraud for the night-boats, and the Surveyors'
  Guild expelled her rather than train her. Keeping this note because the
  guilt angle was useful, but the cartographer version in the dossier is
  cleaner and it's what Book One shipped with.
- Brannic timeline problem: in my first outline Brannic had served the
  Compact a decade, not twenty years, because he needed to be young enough
  to have known Maren Kestrel as a pilot. Check which version Book One
  actually implies before Book Two locks anything.
- What if Tam is Hollow Court? Not a member — a mark. They recruited him
  through the lamp-house months ago and he doesn't know who he's reporting
  to. It would explain how Lash always knows the delve schedule. It would
  also break the only warm thing in the book. Undecided. Maybe the leak
  should stay a Council warden instead. DO NOT seed this until decided.
